# Salesie privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026

Salesie is an AI sales assistant built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. It answers comments and
messages on the **Bloom & Thread** Facebook Page, a demo shop. Nothing is really for sale: checkout links
are test links that never take payment.

This policy explains what Salesie collects when you interact with that Page, why, and how to have it deleted.

## What we collect

When you comment on a Bloom & Thread post or message the Page, Meta sends Salesie:

- your Page-scoped ID (a number Meta assigns for this Page only) and, for comments, your name as Facebook shows it
- the text of your comment or message, and when you sent it

During the conversation Salesie also keeps:

- details you choose to share, such as size, colour, quantity or where you'd like it shipped
- the products you asked about and how far the conversation got (for example, "checkout link sent")
- the replies Salesie and the shop owner sent you

We don't collect card numbers, bank details or other payment information. The test checkout doesn't ask for them.

## How we use it

- to reply to you, answer questions about the products and send you a checkout link when you ask for one
- to let the shop owner see the conversation and reply to you themselves

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it.

## Who processes it

- **Meta** delivers your comments and messages to Salesie and carries our replies back to you on Facebook
  and Messenger.
- **Meta Model API** (the Muse Spark model) writes Salesie's replies. Your messages, the product catalog
  and the conversation so far are sent to it for each reply.
- **Cloudflare** carries the connection between Meta and the server that runs Salesie.

## How long we keep it

This is a hackathon demo. We keep the data only while the demo runs and delete all of it by
31 December 2026.

## Deleting your data

To have everything Salesie holds about you deleted, send the message **"delete my data"** to the
Bloom & Thread Page on Messenger, or open an issue at <https://github.com/akksa/salesie/issues> with your
Facebook name. We'll delete your contact record, conversations and deal history within 30 days and confirm
when it's done.

## Children

Salesie and the Bloom & Thread demo shop are not meant for anyone under 18.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. For questions,
message the Bloom & Thread Page or open an issue at <https://github.com/akksa/salesie/issues>.
